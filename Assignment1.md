# Applying Scrum in a Telemedicine App Project

## Scrum Roles and Responsibilities

### Product Owner
- Defines product vision.
- Prioritizes the Product Backlog.
- Communicates with stakeholders.

### Scrum Master
- Facilitates Scrum events.
- Removes impediments.
- Ensures Scrum practices are followed.

### Development Team
- Builds and tests the product.
- Delivers a working increment every sprint.

## Root Cause Analysis

- Frequent backlog changes during Sprint Planning.
- Poor backlog refinement.
- Lack of clear prioritization.
- Changing stakeholder requirements.

## Improved Backlog Prioritization

- Conduct regular backlog refinement.
- Use the MoSCoW method.
- Apply the Definition of Ready.
- Freeze sprint scope after planning.

## Retrospective Improvements

- Improve stakeholder communication.
- Refine backlog before planning.
- Track sprint metrics.
- Document lessons learned.
